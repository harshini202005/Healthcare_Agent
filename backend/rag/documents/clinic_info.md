# Clinic Information and Policies

## Clinic Hours

The clinic is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The clinic is closed on weekends (Saturday and Sunday) and on public holidays.

Appointment slots are available from 8:00 AM to 5:45 PM in 15-minute intervals (e.g., 8:00, 8:15, 8:30 … 5:30, 5:45).

## Available Specialties

The clinic currently offers the following medical specialties:
- General Practice — Primary care, health checkups, common illnesses
- Cardiology — Heart and cardiovascular conditions
- Dermatology — Skin, hair, and nail conditions
- Neurology — Brain, spinal cord, and nervous system
- Orthopedics — Bones, joints, and muscles
- Pediatrics — Children's health from birth to age 18

## Booking an Appointment

Appointments can be booked through the Healthcare Assistant chatbot or via the Quick Booking form. You will need your Patient ID and preferred date and time.

To book: provide your Patient ID, select a specialty, choose a date and time. If no specific doctor is requested, the system automatically assigns the next available doctor in that specialty.

You will receive a confirmation number (format: APT-XXXXX) after successful booking. Keep this number to manage your appointment.

## Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance. Late cancellations (less than 24 hours notice) or no-shows may affect your ability to book future appointments.

To cancel an appointment, use your confirmation number in the chatbot or contact the clinic reception. Refunds for pre-paid consultations are processed within 5–7 business days.

## What to Bring to Your Appointment

- Government-issued photo ID
- Insurance card (if applicable)
- List of current medications and dosages
- Previous medical records or test results relevant to your visit
- Referral letter (if required by your specialist)

Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to complete any required paperwork.

## Follow-Up and Reminders

The clinic sends automated reminders 24 hours before your appointment. After your visit, the system may schedule a follow-up reminder depending on your treatment plan and the doctor's recommendations.

Patients with chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, heart disease) are encouraged to schedule regular follow-up visits every 3–6 months, as recommended by their doctor.

## Emergency Information

This clinic handles non-emergency medical consultations only. For medical emergencies, please call emergency services (911 in the USA, 999 in the UK, 112 in Europe) or go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately.

Do not use the appointment booking system for urgent conditions such as: chest pain, stroke symptoms, severe difficulty breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, or loss of consciousness.

## Patient Privacy

All patient information is stored securely and handled in accordance with HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) and applicable data protection regulations. Patient data is never shared with third parties without explicit consent except where required by law.

## Common Questions

Q: Can I book an appointment for someone else?
A: Yes. Use the patient's Patient ID when booking on their behalf.

Q: What if I don't know which specialist I need?
A: Book a General Practice appointment. Your GP will assess your condition and refer you to the appropriate specialist if needed.

Q: How early can I book an appointment?
A: You can book up to 90 days in advance. Same-day appointments are available subject to doctor availability.

Q: Is there a waiting list?
A: For high-demand specialties, a waitlist may apply. The system will notify you when a slot opens.

Q: Can I request a specific doctor?
A: Yes. Provide the doctor's ID or name when booking. If your preferred doctor is unavailable at your chosen time, the system can suggest alternative times.

# Medical Specialty Guide: Which Doctor to See

## Cardiology — Heart and Blood Vessels

See a Cardiologist for: chest pain, chest tightness, heart palpitations, shortness of breath at rest or on exertion, high blood pressure (hypertension), irregular heartbeat (arrhythmia), heart attack follow-up, heart failure, high cholesterol, dizziness or fainting with heart symptoms, leg swelling related to heart disease.

Common conditions treated: coronary artery disease, heart failure, atrial fibrillation, hypertension, valvular heart disease, cardiomyopathy.

When to seek urgent care: sudden severe chest pain, pain radiating to arm or jaw, severe breathlessness — these are potential heart attack symptoms, call emergency services immediately.

## Dermatology — Skin, Hair, and Nails

See a Dermatologist for: persistent acne, eczema, psoriasis, suspicious moles or skin growths, rashes that don't clear in 2 weeks, hair loss (alopecia), nail infections or deformities, warts, cysts, skin infections, itchy skin without clear cause, sun damage, skin cancer screening.

Common conditions treated: acne vulgaris, atopic dermatitis, psoriasis, melanoma and other skin cancers, rosacea, vitiligo, fungal infections.

Note: Any mole that changes color, size, or shape should be evaluated promptly by a dermatologist.

## Neurology — Brain, Spinal Cord, and Nerves

See a Neurologist for: recurring headaches or migraines, seizures, memory problems, numbness or tingling in limbs, weakness in arms or legs, tremors, dizziness or vertigo, difficulty with speech or swallowing, multiple sclerosis symptoms, Parkinson's disease, stroke follow-up, neuropathy.

Common conditions treated: migraine, epilepsy, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, stroke, dementia, peripheral neuropathy, Bell's palsy.

When to seek urgent care: sudden severe headache (worst of your life), sudden numbness or weakness on one side, sudden vision loss, sudden confusion or speech difficulty — these may indicate a stroke.

## Orthopedics — Bones, Joints, and Muscles

See an Orthopedic Specialist for: joint pain (knees, hips, shoulders, elbows), back pain, neck pain, sports injuries, fractures, arthritis, tendon or ligament injuries, muscle pain, difficulty walking, scoliosis, bone infections.

Common conditions treated: osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, fractures, rotator cuff tears, ACL/meniscus injuries, herniated discs, carpal tunnel syndrome, osteoporosis.

Note: If you have an acute fracture or dislocation, go to the emergency department first.

## Pediatrics — Children's Health (Ages 0–18)

See a Pediatrician for: newborn and infant checkups, childhood vaccinations, growth and development monitoring, childhood illnesses (fever, ear infections, strep throat), ADHD and learning concerns, childhood asthma, behavioral issues, puberty concerns, school physicals and sports physicals.

Common conditions treated: respiratory infections, asthma, allergies, developmental delays, childhood obesity, ear infections, childhood diabetes.

Well-child visits are recommended at: 1, 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 24 months and annually from age 3 onward.

## General Practice — Primary Care

See a General Practitioner (GP) for: annual health checkups, vaccinations, minor illnesses (cold, flu, UTI), preventive care, chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension), referrals to specialists, blood tests and routine screening, lifestyle advice.

A GP is your first point of contact for most health concerns. They can diagnose and treat many conditions and refer you to a specialist when needed.

## Symptom-to-Specialist Quick Reference

- Chest pain / heart symptoms → Cardiology (or Emergency for acute)
- Skin rash, acne, moles → Dermatology
- Headache, seizures, numbness → Neurology
- Joint pain, broken bone, back pain → Orthopedics
- Child health, vaccinations → Pediatrics
- General illness, checkup, not sure → General Practice
- Stomach pain, digestive issues → General Practice (may refer to Gastroenterology)
- Anxiety, depression, mental health → General Practice (may refer to Psychiatry)
- Diabetes management → General Practice or Endocrinology
- Eye problems → Ophthalmology (not currently available)
- Ear/nose/throat → ENT Specialist (not currently available)
